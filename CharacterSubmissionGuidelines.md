# **The Untold Path | Character Submission Guidelines**

<br>

**The goal is simple: create a character worth writing about.**

<br>

1. **Create an Original Character.**

Characters must be original creations and should not directly copy characters from existing games, movies, books, or other media. Characters should have their own personality, motivations, beliefs, flaws, and goals.

2. **Make Characters Adaptable.**

Characters must be capable of existing across different universes. Avoid making their identity completely dependent on one game's factions, lore, gods, magic systems, locations, or technology. Setting-specific details may be changed to fit future games.

3. **Give Characters Room to Grow.**

Characters should have meaningful flaws, weaknesses, conflicts, and goals. Do not create characters who are perfect, unbeatable, all-knowing, or destined to be the most important person in the world. Leave room for the community to shape their story.

4. **Create Characters That Work Together.**

Untold Path is an ensemble story. Characters should be capable of forming friendships, rivalries, alliances, conflicts, and other relationships with the rest of the cast. Do not control another contributor's character or establish a major relationship.

5. **Focus on the Character, Not the Setting.**

The submission should establish what makes the character who they are. Their personality, beliefs, defining experiences, relationships, motivations, flaws, and goals are more important than extensive setting-specific lore.

6. **Expect Adaptation.**

Characters accepted into the official cast may be adapted when entering a new game. Race, family history, occupation, factions, locations, abilities, and other details may be changed to fit the new universe while preserving the character's core identity.

7. **Keep Submissions Appropriate.**

Character submissions, images, and illustrations must follow the community rules. NSFW, hateful, harassing, or otherwise inappropriate content is not permitted.

8. **Understand the Shared Cast.**

Submitting a character does not guarantee acceptance. Characters must be reviewed, revised, and voted on by the community. Once accepted, characters become part of the official Untold Path cast and may be used and developed as part of the collaborative story.
